The management review module has two parts: quality objectives that you measure period by period, and the reviews themselves, where top management looks at the whole quality system. It follows the usual list of review inputs for ISO 9001.

## Quality objectives

Open **Management review → Quality objectives** and choose **New objective**.

1. Give it a name, an owner, a **Target** and how often it is measured: monthly, quarterly or yearly. The **First period** can be any day. The period it falls in is the first one.
2. Choose **Measured by**. Either a value typed in for each period, or one of four measures Fortress works out from your own records:

| Measure | What it counts | Met when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-conformances raised | Non-conformances raised in the period, leaving out cancelled and deleted ones | At most the target |
| Supplier PPM at goods receipt | Units rejected at goods receipt per million received, across all suppliers | At most the target |
| 8Ds closed on time | Percentage of 8Ds closed in the period by their due date | At least the target |
| Audit pass rate | Percentage of audits completed in the period that passed | At least the target |

For a typed measure you choose whether it is met at least or at most the target, and an optional unit.

The measure, the frequency and the first period are fixed once the objective exists. Each objective page shows where it stands, a chart by period and every value.

**Record a value** is for typed objectives. The objective's owner or a manager does it. Recording a value for a period that already has one makes a correction and needs a note. The old value is kept. System measures are worked out for you each time you open the page, as you. If you may not read the records behind a measure, it says so instead of showing a zero.

**Retire** an objective with a reason when it no longer applies. **Bring back** reverses that.

## Plan a review

Open **Management review → Reviews** and choose **Plan a review**. Enter a title, the period it reviews (up to three years), the **Chair** and, if you know it, a planned date. Use **Change** on the attendees to list members and people from outside, each with a role.

## Hold it

1. Choose **Start the review**. Fortress gathers every input for the period from the modules you may read.
2. For each input section, read the figures, type commentary and tick **Reviewed in the meeting**. **Gather again** refreshes one section, and **Gather everything again** refreshes all of them. A section you may not read shows as not available to you, never as zeros. Someone who can read it should gather it.
3. Mark who was **Present**, **Invited** or sent **Apologies**. Once started, use **Edit** to set the day it was held.
4. Write the **Minutes** in the editor. They save as you type.
5. **Record a decision** (improvement, resources, change to the quality system or other).
6. **Give an action** to a person with a due date, optionally tied to a decision.

The sections are: actions from previous reviews; changes in external and internal issues; customer satisfaction and feedback; quality objectives; process performance and product conformity; non-conformities and corrective actions; monitoring and measurement results; audit results; performance of external providers; adequacy of resources; effectiveness of actions on risks and opportunities; changes to the quality management system; and opportunities for improvement. Changes in context and opportunities for improvement are commentary only.

## Complete it

**Complete** is available when every section is reviewed, someone is recorded as present, the day it was held is set, and there is at least one decision or a note saying why none was needed. A **Still needed to complete** list shows what is left. Once completed, the review is the frozen record: inputs, commentary, attendees, minutes and decisions no longer change. The **Report** button shows the whole review as one page to print or save as PDF.

## Actions

The person an action is given to is told in the app and finds it in **Management review → My actions**, where they choose **Mark done** with what was done. Actions can still be closed after the review is completed. A manager can change, cancel or reopen them. The next review lists the actions from earlier ones.

## Good to know

- Planning, starting, completing and editing need the right to manage management reviews. Anyone given an action can close it.
- A cancelled review cancels its open actions. Nothing is deleted.
- Actions that are due or late also appear in your daily reminders. How you are told follows your choices in **Settings → Notifications** (see [Notifications and reminders](/help/notifications-and-reminders)).
- The home page shows the next review, the last one completed and open actions.
