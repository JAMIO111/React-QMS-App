Fortress is billed through Stripe: **one organisation fee**, plus **a seat for each person** who uses it. Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by Fortress. Prices exclude VAT, which is added where it applies.

## What it costs

| | Monthly | Annual (two months free) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organisation fee**, one per organisation | £99 | £990 |
| **Controller** seat | £49 per person | £490 per person |
| **Contributor** seat | £19 per person | £190 per person |
| **Operator** seat | £6 per person | £60 per person |
| **Observer** seat | Free, up to twice your paid seats | Free |

The organisation fee covers every module, support and the platform itself, however many seats you have. It is not a seat and nobody holds it.

**Free Observers.** You can have up to twice as many Observer seats as paid seats (Controller, Contributor and Operator together). With one Controller and four Operators you can add up to ten Observers. To add more, add a paid seat.

## The four seat packages

| Package | What a person can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Observer** | Reads dashboards, registers, documents and alerts, and acknowledges what is addressed to them. |
| **Operator** | The shop floor: records inspections and jobs and raises non-conformances, on top of what an Observer can do. Elsewhere they read. |
| **Contributor** | Does the work: raises and progresses non-conformances, runs 8Ds, and records calibrations, audits and deliveries. |
| **Controller** | Signs off and sets up: approves dispositions, verifies and closes 8Ds, issues documents, approves suppliers and runs integrations. |

A person's role and their seat work together. The role says what they are allowed to do; the seat decides how far they can go. A role never gives more than the seat allows: an Operator who also has a quality manager role still cannot edit a non-conformance after raising it. Administration rights such as members, roles, security and billing need no seat. See [Roles and permissions](/help/roles-and-permissions).

## Starting your subscription

In the set-up wizard (or **Settings → Subscription & billing**), choose how many seats of each package you need. The total is shown with the organisation fee before you pay. You are then taken to a Stripe checkout page. A card is needed to start the **14-day trial**, and you pay nothing until it ends. You can also choose to be invoiced instead, if Fortress has allowed it for your organisation.

An organisation needs at least one **Controller** seat, held by an owner.

## Giving people seats

Seats are given on **Settings → Users & permissions**, either when you invite someone or later from their card. A person's seat changes what they can open straight away.

## Changing seats

On **Settings → Subscription & billing** you can change the number of seats. Before you confirm, a preview from Stripe shows what the change will cost. Extra seats are charged at once for the rest of the period; fewer seats are credited on your next invoice. You cannot go below the number of seats in use, and you cannot go over the free Observer limit.

## If you subscribed before the new prices

Subscriptions started before November 2026 keep the prices they started on, and they have no organisation fee. Seats you add to a package you already pay for are charged at your existing price. A package you did not have before is charged at today's price.

## Manage billing

**Manage billing** opens Stripe's own portal, where you can update your card, see invoices and cancel.

## If something goes wrong

- If a payment fails, a banner tells you the date access will stop. You can update your card from the same page.
- If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
- Until a valid subscription is in place, you can still reach **Settings** to fix it.
