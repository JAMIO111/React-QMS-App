Everyone who uses Fortress needs three things: a **role** (what they may do), a **place** (a site and departments) and a **seat** (which parts of the product they can use).

## Send an invitation

Go to **Settings → Users & permissions** and fill in the invite form:

- **Email address** of the person.
- **Role.** Owner, admin, quality manager, quality engineer, inspector or viewer. Owners and admins manage the account itself.
- **Limited to site.** Leave it empty for all sites, or restrict the role to one.
- **Departments.** Required unless every role you give is owner, admin or quality manager. Tick the person's departments and mark one as primary.
- **Seat.** Reserved for them as soon as you send the invitation.
- **A message**, if you would like to add one.

To invite many people at once, use the team step of the set-up wizard: paste addresses, set a role for everyone or per person, and see the result for each row.

## What the person receives

A new address gets an email with a link to set a password and accept. Someone who already has a Fortress account gets a sign-in link instead. Invitations last 14 days.

On the people list you can **Resend** an invitation (not more than once a minute, up to ten times) or **Revoke** it, which frees the reserved seat.

## Their first sign-in

A person who joins an existing organisation sees a short welcome: a few details about them, how they would like to be notified, and where to start for their role.

## Seats and waiting

Where seats are enforced, someone without a seat can sign in but cannot use the product. They see who can give them a seat and when those people were told, and they can send one reminder a day. People who manage seats are notified when someone is waiting.

## Change someone later

On the same page each person is a card. You can add or remove roles and departments, move their primary department, change their seat, suspend or reactivate them, or remove them. Changes show at once.
