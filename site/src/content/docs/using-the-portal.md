A company you work with has given you access to its quality portal. You see what it chooses to share with your company, and you can reply, respond and approve where it asks.

## Sign in and accept

Open the invitation email and follow the link. If you have no account for that address you set a password, and you may be asked to set up two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. Then open the portal. Under **You have been invited**, choose **Accept**. An expired invitation needs a new one from whoever invited you, and the invitation only matches the email address it was sent to.

If you work for more than one customer or supplier, choose the organisation you want.

## Find your way

The **Dashboard** shows what needs your attention. Suppliers see their performance. Customers see figures on the quality of what they buy. The side menu depends on who you are:

| Menu item | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Non-conformances | Everyone |
| Corrective actions | Suppliers |
| 8D reports | Customers |
| Complaints | Customers |
| Part approvals | Suppliers and customers |
| Change requests (customers) or Process changes (suppliers) | Both |
| Documents and alerts | Everyone |
| Certificates and reports | Customers |
| Team | Admins only |

The registers have search, filters, sorting and CSV export. Badges show what is waiting.

## Shared records

Open a record to read it and join the **Conversation**. You see the problem, the actions and the decisions. You do not see costs, internal notes or the people who work there. If your contact asks **Can this be closed?**, choose yes, or say what is still wrong.

## Corrective action requests (suppliers)

Open a request under **Corrective actions**. Choose **Acknowledge we have it** if you wish. **Respond** opens a form that needs a root cause and a corrective action. You can add why it was not caught, where else it could happen, containment, preventive action, a planned date and a responsible person. Add evidence files with **Add a file** before you send. Files you added can be taken back until the response is sent.

Under **Actions we ask of you**, choose **Complete** and say what was done. **Ask for more time** sends a request. The date moves only if they approve it, and you can take the request back. Your response is reviewed by them: it may be accepted, or you may be asked to **Send a revised response**.

## Documents and alerts

Open one and read it. Choose **Acknowledge**, with an optional note. It is recorded once for you. A new revision asks again.

## Complaints (customers)

Choose **Raise a complaint**: a title, what is wrong, and optionally the part, how many, the lot, your reference and the date you found it. It is received as a draft for them to review. You can add files while it is still received. After that, talk to them in the shared record.

## Part approvals and changes

When asked, open **Part approvals** and choose **Approve it**, **Approve it for now** (with a date and conditions) or **I cannot accept it**, with a reason. Suppliers add files to each element asked of them and choose **Submit the package**. Customers can send a change request, and suppliers a process change notice, from their change menu item. A customer can also approve a change they are asked about.

## Good to know

- You see only your own company's records.
- Team admins invite and remove their own colleagues under **Team**.
- You cannot see other suppliers or customers, or how your company is ranked unless the organisation turned that on.
