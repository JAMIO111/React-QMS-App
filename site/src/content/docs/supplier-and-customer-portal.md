The portal lets people at a supplier or customer sign in to your organisation without becoming members. They have no role or seat and see nothing except what you share. This guide is for your side. [Using the portal](/help/using-the-portal) covers theirs.

## Switch it on

Open **Settings → External access**. Owners, administrators and quality managers can see it.

1. Turn on **Supplier and customer portal**. Until you do, nobody outside can sign in.
2. Leave **Require two-factor authentication for external people** on unless you have a reason. These accounts sit outside your company.
3. Under **Who can use it**, turn on each supplier or customer who should have access. A party that is off sees nothing.

## Invite their people

Choose **People** beside a party, then **Invite someone**: an email address, a role and an optional message. Choose **Admin** for the first contact. An admin can invite and remove their own colleagues from the portal's **Team** page, so you do not have to. **Member** can use the portal but not manage people.

An invitation lasts seven days. You can send it again, suspend someone or remove them with a reason. You cannot invite an address that belongs to one of your own people. **Your contact, as they see it** sets who they are told to talk to; empty shows your organisation's name. The **Access report** lists everyone and their last sign-in.

## Share records

People with the right to share (owners, administrators, quality managers and quality engineers) get a **Shared with suppliers and customers** card on:

- a **non-conformance**, with the supplier or customer it names;
- an **8D**, with a party named by one of its linked non-conformances;
- a **document** in force, and a **quality alert** in force, with any party that has the portal on.

Choose **Share with**, pick the party, then **Share**. The card then offers:

- **Conversation:** both sides talk on the record. Staff appear as your organisation, not by name. Anyone can withdraw their own comment within ten minutes.
- **Closure** (customers): **Ask them to confirm** that a non-conformance or 8D can be closed. They confirm or say what is still wrong. Nothing closes automatically.
- **Acknowledged by** (documents and alerts): who has acknowledged. A new revision asks again.
- **Stop sharing**, with a reason.

They see a curated view of each record, not the record itself. Costs, internal notes and your own people are never shown.

## Other things the portal does

- **SCARs:** a supplier sees every SCAR once it is issued, with no sharing step. They respond, ask for more time and complete your actions. See [Supplier corrective actions](/help/supplier-corrective-actions).
- **Complaints:** a customer raises one, and it arrives as a draft non-conformance with the source customer. Severity, category and site stay empty for you to decide. Switch this off with **Options** beside the customer.
- **Change requests:** customers can ask for a change and suppliers can notify a process change. Each arrives as a draft change. You can also put a change to a customer for approval from its page. See [Change management](/help/change-management).
- **Part approvals:** put a sent PPAP to the customer to answer, or ask a supplier to prepare a package. See [PPAP](/help/ppap).
- **Certificates and reports:** in a lot's dialog under **Production → Lots**, **Share a file** gives one customer a certificate or report.
- **Performance:** **Tiles** beside a supplier chooses which figures they see. The ranking switch shows only a band, never names or numbers, once five suppliers have a score.

## Good to know

- You are told when a party replies, acknowledges or responds. Comments appear on the record.
- Email goes to a party's people when you share or reply, if email is set up. It is sent once and never retried.
- Files on a shared non-conformance or 8D, and a customer seeing records you have not shared, are not built. (Files do travel on SCAR responses, complaints and lot certificates.)
