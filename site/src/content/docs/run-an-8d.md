An 8D is a structured corrective action. Fortress keeps one record per 8D with nine steps, D0 to D8, and finds the stage from the steps rather than letting anyone set it.

## Start one

Open a non-conformance and choose **Start a new 8D** in the Investigation card. The 8D is prefilled from the NC. You can also start one from **Corrective Actions**, or link an NC to an existing 8D later.

## The steps

You can work on the steps in any order.

- **D0 Initiate.** Title, owner, due date, severity and the linked NCs.
- **D1 Team.** Members from your organisation or external contacts. One leader and one champion.
- **D2 Problem.** The 5W2H questions (who, what, where, when, why, how, how many) and an Is / Is-Not analysis.
- **D3 Containment.** Interim actions, or a reason none are needed.
- **D4 Root cause.** Ishikawa and 5 Whys tools. At least one occurrence cause is needed, and every cause must be **verified** with a method before the step is done.
- **D5 Corrective actions.** Actions linked to the causes they fix.
- **D6 Implement and verify.** Do the actions, then record a verification.
- **D7 Prevent.** Preventive actions or a reason none are needed, plus lessons learned.
- **D8 Close.** Closes the 8D once everything before it is complete and the fix is verified effective.

Each step has a short **still needed** list at the bottom.

## Completed steps lock

Completing a step makes it read-only. Reopening it needs a reason and touches only that step. This is what stops a finished investigation being rewritten.

## Actions and who does them

Managers write an action and assign it to a team member. The person it is assigned to can then record their findings and close or reopen their own action, even with read-only access to 8Ds. They cannot change what it is, who has it or when it is due. **My actions** under Corrective Actions lists theirs.

## Verification

Someone with permission to verify records whether the corrective actions worked. If the actions change afterwards, that verification is marked **stale** and a new one is needed. A failed verification reopens D5 and D6.

## Report

The **Report** link in the 8D header shows the whole 8D as one document. Use **Print or save as PDF** to share it.
