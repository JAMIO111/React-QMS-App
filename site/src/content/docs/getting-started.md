Fortress QMS runs in your browser. There is nothing to install. This guide takes you from sign-up to a set-up organisation.

## Create your account

1. Choose **Start free trial** on the website, then enter your name, work email and a password. The sign-up form shows the password rules as you type.
2. Tick the box to accept the terms and privacy policy.
3. Check your inbox for the confirmation email and open the link. If it has not arrived, the page has a **Resend** button.

Some email systems open every link before you do, which can use up a one-time link. Fortress links land on a page with a **Continue** button for that reason, so the link survives.

## Start your organisation

After confirming you reach **Start your organisation**. If someone has already invited you, their invitation is listed here to accept. Otherwise create your organisation: a name, your first site, its time zone and your country.

## Work through the wizard

The set-up wizard walks you through the choices that matter first:

- **Plan.** Choose how many seats of each package you need and start your trial. See [Billing and seats](/help/billing-and-seats).
- **Sites and departments.** Add each site and the departments inside it. People are placed in departments, and records belong to sites.
- **Company details and security.** Your legal name and country, and whether everyone must use two-factor sign-in.
- **Your team.** Paste a list of email addresses and give each person a role, department and seat. See [Invite your team](/help/invite-your-team).

You can choose **Finish later** at any step. The home page keeps an **onboarding card** showing what is left until the wizard is finished.

## The go-live checklist

Once the wizard is finished, the home page shows a short checklist until the essentials are in place:

- A valid subscription, a site, a department and someone invited are **required**.
- A legal name and country, a logo, a security decision, someone who has joined and a first non-conformance are **recommended**.

## Make it yours

In **Settings → Organisation** you can upload a logo and pick a theme colour. Both appear for everyone in your organisation.

## Next steps

Invite your colleagues, then try [raising a non-conformance](/help/raise-a-non-conformance) or [importing your parts](/help/import-your-data).
