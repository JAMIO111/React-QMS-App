Fortress is billed per seat through Stripe. Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by Fortress.

## The three packages

| Package | What a person can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Observer** | View. Read records, dashboards and reports. |
| **Contributor** | Operate. Record work such as readings, calibrations and audit answers, on top of viewing. |
| **Controller** | Manage. Configure modules, approve, and manage integrations, on top of everything else. |

A person's role and their package work together. The role says what they are allowed to do; the package says which parts of the product they can use.

## Starting your subscription

In the set-up wizard (or **Settings → Subscription & billing**) choose how many seats of each package you need. You are taken to a Stripe checkout page. A card is needed to start the **14-day trial**. Prices are shown when you choose your seats, before you pay.

An organisation needs at least one **Controller** seat, held by an owner.

## Giving people seats

Seats are assigned on **Settings → Users & permissions**, either when you invite someone or later from their card. A person's seat changes what they can open straight away.

## Changing seats

On **Settings → Subscription & billing** you can change the number of seats. A preview from Stripe shows what the change will cost before you confirm it. You cannot go below the number of seats already in use.

## Manage billing

**Manage billing** opens Stripe's own portal, where you can update your card, see invoices and cancel.

## If something goes wrong

- If a payment fails, a banner tells you the date access will stop, and you can update your card from the same page.
- If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
- Until a valid subscription is in place, you can still reach **Settings** to fix it.
