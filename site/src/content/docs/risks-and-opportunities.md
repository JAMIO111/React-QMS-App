The risk register holds both risks and opportunities in one list. Each is scored on a likelihood and consequence matrix, treated, and reviewed on a schedule. It records and prompts. It never stops work.

## Raise one

Open **Risk → Risk register** and choose **Raise a risk**. Say whether it is a risk or an opportunity, then give a category, a title, an optional cause and effect, and an owner. A site and department are optional. The kind is fixed once it has been assessed.

You can also raise one from the record it came from. A **Risks** card on a non-conformance, on step D7 of an 8D, on an audit finding, on a supplier profile and on a machine shows the risks linked to it and offers **Raise a risk**. The title, description, category and links are suggested from that record, and you can take any suggested link off. A risk keeps a link to where it was raised from, and that link cannot be removed. Use **Change links** on the risk to add parts, suppliers, machines, documents, 8Ds or operations.

## Assess it

A new risk is a draft until it has an assessment. Choose **Assess it**, click a cell of the matrix and say **Why this score?**. Fortress works out the score and band from your organisation's matrix, and the page shows it. Each assessment is kept. Assessing again replaces the one in force and counts as a review.

## Decide what to do

1. Under **Treatment**, choose **Choose the approach**: Mitigate, Avoid, Transfer or Accept for a risk, or Exploit or Leave for an opportunity. Add a short plan.
2. Use **Add an action** for each step, given to a person with an optional due date. The person it is given to can mark it done, with what was done, even if they can only read the register.
3. Once the actions are in hand, use **Assess again** under **After treatment** to record what is left. Any approach other than Accept or Leave needs that second assessment before the risk reaches Monitoring.

The status is worked out, not set by hand: Draft, Open (assessed), Treating (an approach chosen), then Monitoring when nothing more is needed. The **Still needed** box says what is missing.

## Acceptance

If the level in force is above what your organisation accepts, the risk reaches Monitoring, or closes as accepted, only when an approver agrees. Choose **Propose accepting it** with a reason of at least ten characters. A different person with approval rights then chooses **Decide** to approve or reject. The acceptance covers that assessment only. Assessing again asks afresh. Opportunities need no acceptance.

## Review and close

The next review date comes from the review interval set for the band the risk is in. Choose **Record a review** to say what you looked at, with an optional next date. A review that is late shows on the risk and in the register.

**Close** a risk as Treated, Accepted or No longer applies (for an opportunity: Realised, Left or No longer applies), with a note. Its open actions are cancelled. **Reopen** brings it back, due for review that day. **Retire** takes a risk off the live register but needs its open actions closed or cancelled first.

## See the picture

- **Risk → Dashboard** counts live risks, unassessed ones, high risks with nothing done, overdue reviews and open actions.
- **Risk → Heat map** shows how many risks sit in each cell. Switch between **In force**, **Before treatment** and **After treatment**, filter by risk or opportunity, category, or only your own, and choose a cell to list its risks.
- **Risk → My risk work** lists actions given to you, reviews due and acceptances waiting for your decision. The same count shows as a badge on the menu.

## Set up the matrix

Under **Settings → Data Management → Risk** you can see the 5 by 5 matrix, its bands, the acceptable level and the review interval for each band. Approvers use **Change the matrix** to move bands and labels, giving a reason. That makes a new version. Earlier assessments keep the version they were scored with. Categories can be added, renamed and switched off.

## Good to know

- You need the right to manage risks to raise, assess and treat them. Only approvers decide acceptance and change the matrix.
- Risks can be linked to an [FMEA](/help/fmea) cause and feed the [management review](/help/management-review).
- People given a risk or an action are told in the app. Reviews and actions that are due or overdue also appear in your daily reminders (see [Notifications and reminders](/help/notifications-and-reminders)).
