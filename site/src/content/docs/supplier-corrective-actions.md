A SCAR (supplier corrective action request) asks an outside company to investigate and fix a problem, and tracks what comes back. It is not your internal 8D, though it can link to one. Find them under **Supplier Quality → Supplier corrective actions**, on a supplier's **SCARs** tab, or from a supplier's non-conformance.

## Raise and issue one

Choose **Raise a SCAR**. Pick the supplier and the non-conformances it is about (only that supplier's own). Choosing a non-conformance fills the title, problem and requirement breached. Add the requirement breached, the evidence, the containment you need, a response due date and an internal owner. It starts as a draft.

The same button is on a supplier's non-conformance page, with the supplier and non-conformance already chosen. A retry never makes a second one.

**Issue to the supplier** needs a due date in the future and at least one linked non-conformance. After that, only the contact and 8D link can change.

## The statuses

Draft, Issued, Acknowledged, Response received, Under review, Revision requested, Accepted, Verifying effectiveness, Closed or Cancelled. The page shows five stages and a **Next** card offering only what you may do.

## Response and review

- If the supplier uses the portal they respond there. Otherwise choose **Record a response**. A root cause and a corrective action are required.
- **Record acknowledgement** is optional.
- A response never closes the SCAR. **Start the review**, then **Accept the response** or **Ask for a revision**, with a note. A revision asks for a new response.
- **Record implementation** once the fix is carried out.
- **Verify effectiveness** needs approval rights. Effective closes the SCAR. Not effective sends it back for a revision.

## More time

**Request more time** records a request, and the date does not move yet. Someone with approval rights, who did not ask, chooses **Decide**. Approval moves the due date and keeps the original. A recorded response or a cancellation withdraws a waiting request.

## Affected items and actions

**Affected items** lists the parts, materials, lots and deliveries involved. Add or remove them with a reason while the SCAR runs.

**Actions for the supplier** lets you ask them to do something by a date. They complete each one in their portal with a note. You can edit, cancel or reopen an action with a reason. Actions never change the status. See [Giving suppliers and customers access](/help/supplier-and-customer-portal).

## Overdue

A SCAR is overdue when a response is awaited (Issued, Acknowledged or Revision requested) and the response due date has passed. This is worked out when you look, and the page says how many days late. The register has an **Overdue** view.

## Good to know

- **Cancel SCAR** needs a reason. A cancelled SCAR is kept.
- Reopening a closed SCAR and editing a recorded response are not built; a revision is a new response.
- Fortress can email the supplier's portal people when you issue, review or decide a date. It never undoes the step if the email fails.
