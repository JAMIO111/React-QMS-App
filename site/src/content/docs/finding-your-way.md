Fortress has one side menu, one home page and the same register tools everywhere. Learn them once and every module feels familiar.

## The side menu

Every module is a group in the side menu. You only see the modules your role and your organisation's seat package allow, so your menu may be shorter than a colleague's. Some groups show a count badge for work waiting for you.

| Group | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard, My tasks | The home page and tasks that workflow rules gave you |
| Non-Conformance, Corrective Actions (8D) | The NC dashboard and register, 8Ds and your 8D actions |
| In-Process Inspection | Jobs and inspections |
| Process Control, Calibration | Control plans, SPC charts and alerts, gauges, Gauge R&R |
| Audits, Documentation | Audit schedule, templates, records and findings, then documents, change requests and quality alerts |
| Supplier Quality, Shipping | Suppliers, orders, receiving, supplier corrective actions, then customer orders and shipments |
| Competence, Risk, Change, FMEA, PPAP, Management review | The quality system modules |
| Production, Master Data | Orders, lots, serial numbers and traceability, then parts, machines, customers, suppliers and employees |

Settings are in the profile menu at the top right.

## The home page

Open **Dashboard** to see what needs attention across every area you may read, as of now. People setting up the organisation also see set-up cards at the top, and people who record inspections see **Start new job**.

- **Needs attention** counts critical items (late, failed or quarantined) and items to watch (waiting or due soon).
- **Act on these first** lists the critical items, largest first. Each line opens the register already filtered.
- **For you** shows your own items, such as quality alerts to acknowledge, documents to read or sign off, and 8D actions assigned to you.
- **By area** gives a card per area with its headline figure and its lines, or **All clear**.

An area you may not read is left out, not shown as zero.

## Two ways to search

The bar at the top says **Filter this page…**. What you type narrows the register or list on screen as you type.

To jump somewhere else, press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) or use the **Ctrl K** button in the bar. The command palette starts empty and shows what you opened lately. Type to find:

- **Pages**: anything in the side menu or Settings that you may open.
- **Actions**, such as **New non-conformance** or **Start an inspection**, if you may do them.
- **Records**: non-conformances, 8Ds, parts, suppliers, gauges, inspections and documents, from two letters. Kinds you may not read do not appear.

Use the arrow keys and Enter to choose, and Esc to close. Your text in the top bar is left alone.

## Registers

Registers share the same table tools.

- Click a column heading to sort. Drag its right edge to resize it; double-click the edge to reset.
- The **Columns** button shows and hides columns, reorders them and resets them. You can also drag headings. Your choices are remembered in your browser for that register.
- The filter drawer sets filters. Chosen filters show as chips you can remove.
- Tick rows to see totals and, where the register offers it, **Export CSV**.

## Saved filters

The non-conformance, FMEA, part approval and supplier registers have a bar above the table with **All** and your own saved filters. One click applies one.

1. Choose filters in the drawer.
2. Choose **Save filter** and give it a name.
3. Use the **…** button to rename or delete.

You can keep five per register. Saving under an existing name replaces that filter. They are private to you.

## Tasks, notifications and your profile

- **My tasks** lists tasks workflow rules gave you. See [Workflows and tasks](/help/workflows-and-tasks).
- The bell opens your notifications. Filter by **All**, **New** or **Read**, or choose **Mark all as read**. See [Notifications and reminders](/help/notifications-and-reminders).
- The profile menu holds **My reminders**, **Settings**, **Help**, **Dark mode** and **Logout**.

## Good to know

Menu counts refresh about once a minute. Dashboards stay fresh for a minute and update when you act.
