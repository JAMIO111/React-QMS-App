This policy explains what we store on your device when you use this website and the Fortress QMS application.

## This website

The marketing website sets **no cookies** and uses **no analytics, advertising or tracking** tools. Its fonts are served from the site itself, not from a third party. Because we do not use non-essential cookies on the website, we do not show a cookie banner.

## The application

The application uses browser storage that is **strictly necessary** to work, or that remembers choices you made:

| What | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in session (kept in your browser's local storage by our sign-in provider) | To keep you signed in and prove who you are to the service | Until you sign out or the session ends |
| Interface preferences: theme, table columns, sizes and order, recently used machine, dismissed notices | To remember how you like the screens | Until you clear them |
| Short-lived choices kept for the current visit, such as the seats you were choosing before checkout | To carry your choice across a page change | Until you close the tab |

These are needed to provide a service you asked for, so they do not need your consent. You can clear them in your browser settings, but you will be signed out and your preferences will reset.

## Third parties

When you start a subscription you are sent to Stripe's checkout page, which may set its own cookies. See Stripe's policies for details.

## If this changes

If we add analytics or anything else that is not strictly necessary we will update this page and ask for your consent first.
